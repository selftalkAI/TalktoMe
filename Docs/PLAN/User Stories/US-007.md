# US-007 — Brain 1 Core Agents and Knowledge Base

**Status:** Ready
**Priority:** MVP / must-have (MVP cores only)
**Related requirements:** `FSD FR-B1-002`, `FR-B1-008` – `FR-B1-011`
**Related architecture:** `ADD §8.2`, `ADD ADR-018`, `ADD ADR-020`, `TDD §3`, `TDD §5.4`
**Spec:** `Docs/FEATURES/Building_Brain1.md` §7, §9, §10

## Story

As a user,
I want selfie.Me to understand what's really going on in my life — not just the words of my last message — using real expertise from day one,
so that its advice is knowledgeable and right for me.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** any input, **when** Brain 1 runs, **then** the Safety Core runs first; `crisis` stops coaching and returns care with vetted resources.
- **Given** "Couldn't go to the gym, kids were sick again", **when** Brain 1 runs, **then** it routes to Body, Relationships and Mind, and the Body area summary attributes the miss to caregiving rather than motivation when the evidence supports it.
- **Given** a core agent investigating, **when** it runs, **then** it stays within its step and cost limits and every tool call is traced.
- **Given** the book library, **when** principle cards are distilled, **then** each card cites its book and chapter and is reviewed before use.
- **Given** book search, **when** it runs, **then** ranking uses similarity only (no recency).
- **Given** the user's next message after a reply, **when** scored, **then** an outcome is recorded against the persona and principles used.
