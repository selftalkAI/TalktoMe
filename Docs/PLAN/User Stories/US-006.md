# US-006 — Brain 1 Profile and Context Pack

**Status:** Ready
**Priority:** MVP / must-have
**Related requirements:** `FSD FR-B1-001`, `FR-B1-003` – `FR-B1-007`, `FR-PROF-001`
**Related architecture:** `ADD §6.1`, `ADD ADR-017`, `TDD §4`, `TDD §21`
**Spec:** `Docs/FEATURES/Building_Brain1.md` §8, §11

## Story

As a user,
I want selfie.Me to keep a clear, structured picture of who I am — my people, places, tastes, routines, goals, and what we've talked about — and use the right parts of it in every reply,
so that I never have to repeat myself and every answer fits my life today.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a user, **when** their profile is built, **then** it has the 12 sections plus Here & Now, and every field records source, evidence, confidence, status and tier.
- **Given** the user mentions a new fact ("Mira's school is closed for snow"), **when** the turn is remembered, **then** the fact is routed to its owning section (People, Here & Now) with evidence.
- **Given** an inferred fact, **when** stored, **then** it is a hypothesis (`requires_confirmation`) until the user confirms it.
- **Given** a turn, **when** the Context Pack is compiled, **then** it contains only relevant sections, the last conversation's summary and open threads, what works / avoid, hooks with their sources, and unknowns — and no T3 item without opt-in.
- **Given** the user corrects a fact, **when** the next reply is composed, **then** the corrected value is used and the old one is superseded.
- **Given** any profile change, **when** saved, **then** a new profile version is recorded.
