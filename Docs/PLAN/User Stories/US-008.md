# US-008 — Personas: Voice × Expertise × Stance

**Status:** Ready
**Priority:** MVP / should-have (first voices), Post-MVP (full set)
**Related requirements:** `FSD FR-PER-001` – `FR-PER-007`, `BR-017`, `BR-019`
**Related architecture:** `ADD ADR-021`, `ADD §9`
**Spec:** `Docs/FEATURES/Building_Brain2.md` §5; `Building_Brain1.md` §12.4

## Story

As a user,
I want selfie.Me to talk to me the way I need in the moment — like a friend, a big sister, a mentor, or a coach — with the right know-how,
so that it feels like the right person is there for me, not a generic assistant.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a turn, **when** Brain 1 picks a persona, **then** it chooses a voice, an expertise and a stance, and records them.
- **Given** the user says "talk to me like my sister", **when** later replies are composed, **then** they use the big-sister voice until the user changes it.
- **Given** the user's People section records a parent's death, **when** a persona is picked, **then** the matching parent-like voice is never used.
- **Given** the user asks "are you a real person?", **when** Brain 2 replies in any persona, **then** it says it is an AI.
- **Given** a money question about a specific investment, **when** the financial expertise answers, **then** it gives general guidance and suggests a licensed advisor.
- **Given** Safety returns `concern` or `crisis`, **when** a persona was chosen, **then** it is overridden by a gentle listening voice / care response.
