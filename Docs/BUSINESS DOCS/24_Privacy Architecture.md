# Privacy Architecture

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 24*

Define privacy as a core product capability.

## Principles

- Data minimization.
- User ownership.
- Encryption in transit/at rest.
- Strict user-scoped authorization.
- Explicit trusted-access permissions.
- Export and deletion.
- Separation of evidence and inference.
- The Profile is visible only to its owning user by default. No mechanism in this document authorizes a second person — including a family member, and including posthumous access — to read it; any future "legacy" access is a distinct, dedicated consent and legal decision (`27_Consent and AI Identity Policy`), never an extension of existing sharing, export, or family-plan controls.

## Privacy UX

Users should be able to see what selfie.Me remembers, why it remembers it, who can access it, how to remove it, and — for any Profile proposal — what Brain 2 compared their input against (the outside-knowledge source).

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.

## Basis

Grounded in the selfie.Me Strategic Framework and Business Model Canvas, the founder's clarified direction, and the Brain 1 / Brain 2 design in `Docs/FEATURES/Building_Brain1.md` and `Building_Brain2.md`, formalized in the Architecture, Functional and Technical Design Documents (V03).
