# Business Model Canvas

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 02*

Define how selfie.Me creates, delivers, and captures value.

## Customer Segments

- Initial: founder/user #1 and digitally comfortable adults who want to understand themselves over time.
- Later: parents/families, professionals preserving knowledge, older adults documenting life history.
- Aspirational, not yet buildable: trusted legacy recipients — see "Legacy" note below.

## Value Proposition

- Talk naturally; selfie.Me remembers — but only once you've actually reflected on it, never from raw input alone.
- A living model of you — your Brain 1 Persona — that **integrates** the scattered pieces of your complete human persona (who you are, your people, places, tastes, goals, skills, feelings, story) into one structured, evolving Profile, starting from day one with expert knowledge distilled from trusted books.
- A voice — Brain 2 — that **enhances** your life by talking to you the way you need right now (a friend, a big sister, a mentor, a coach…), with the right know-how, using everything your Persona knows — and following through with well-timed check-ins so change takes hold in your life, not just in your data.
- See how opinions, goals, priorities and decisions evolved.
- Receive reflections grounded in your own history rather than generic advice.
- Maintain ownership, privacy, export, deletion and trusted-access controls.

## Channels

iOS/Android app, web app, direct web acquisition, referrals, creator storytelling.

## Revenue

- Freemium capture/search.
- Premium personal intelligence, larger memory capacity, and Profile refinement across more domains.
- Family/trusted-access plans (shared memory features only — not Profile access by another person).
- Legacy offering — not yet scheduled: see note below.

## Key Resources

Agentic AI (Brain 1's 13 core agents and Brain 2's persona-shaped reply pipeline — see `12_Agentic AI Architecture`), LLMs, speech-to-text, embeddings, ML/neural models, a personal knowledge graph, a World Knowledge lookup, secure storage, identity and privacy infrastructure.

## Costs

AI inference, speech, storage, databases, vector retrieval, outside-knowledge lookups, engineering, security, legal/privacy, app-store/payment fees.

## Legacy Note

A future digital legacy offering — letting a second person (e.g. a family member) query a user's Profile, potentially after the user's death — remains a real long-term goal but is explicitly unbuilt and undecided: it requires a dedicated consent and legal framework that does not exist yet. It is tracked as an open founder decision, not a roadmap commitment (`27_Consent and AI Identity Policy`).

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.
