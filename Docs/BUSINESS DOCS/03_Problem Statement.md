# Problem Statement

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 03*

Define the human problem before defining features.

## Core Problem

People continuously form opinions, make decisions, change priorities, learn lessons and forget why they once thought or acted a certain way. Existing notes and chat histories store fragments but rarely maintain a coherent, evidence-backed understanding of how a person evolves.

## User Pain

- Important thoughts disappear into chat logs, notes and memory.
- Past reasoning is hard to retrieve when making a similar decision.
- People remember conclusions but forget context and causes.
- Generic AI advice does not know the person's longitudinal history, and does not check the person's own approach against outside expertise before offering an opinion.

## Opportunity

Create a private second brain — a Brain 1 Persona that knows me and a Brain 2 voice that talks to me the way I need — that **integrates** the scattered pieces of a person's complete human persona — skill, emotion, relationships, career, learning, habits, and every other part of a life — into one coherent, standing Profile: what I thought, why I thought it, what changed, what happened. Brain 2 then **enhances** my life by comparing it against the outside world's expertise, proposing what I might consider or change, and — once I accept it — following through with ongoing support so the change actually takes hold, not just a one-time piece of advice.

The mechanism is deliberately narrow: a thought only becomes *eligible* for this once the user's own reflection is layered onto it — an article merely read, or help received in a hard moment, is not itself a memory, it becomes one only when the user's own thinking lands on top of it. But reflection alone isn't enough to make something durable: it still has to be proposed back to the user and explicitly accepted before it becomes part of the Profile.

## Non-Goal

The initial product is not an autonomous clone that impersonates the user, and Brain 2 never acts or decides on Brain 1's behalf — it proposes, and only the user's acceptance makes anything durable.

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.
