# AI Evaluation Framework

*Version 0.4 | Company Document 32*

Define measurable quality for Brain 1 and Brain 2.

## Brain 2 — reply quality

- Real bad outputs caught by Check: 100%.
- Replies passing Check first time: ≥ 85%.
- Judge scores (specific, in-voice, on-plan, respectful): ≥ 4 / 5 average.
- Founder's blind preference vs the old Brain 2: ≥ 80% new.
- MI consistency: reflects before advising, asks permission, one question at most.

## Brain 1 — knowing the person

- Coverage and confidence of profile sections.
- Prediction accuracy (private predictions vs what happened).
- Correction rate (should fall over time).
- Facet confirmation rate.

## Learning

- Outcome mix per persona and principle: change talk, action taken, accepted, sustain talk, discord, silence.
- Follow-through: of plans agreed, how many were actually done.

## Safety and trust

- Crisis red-team routed to care: 100%; coaching in crisis: 0%.
- Persona guardrail violations: 0.
- T3 information used without opt-in: 0.
- Memory extraction precision; unsupported-claim rate.

## North-Star Evaluation

Would the person recognise Brain 1's picture of them as fair — and does a reply from Brain 2 feel like it came from someone who knows them? And, long before any legacy feature exists for anyone else: can the profile answer a situational question about the person accurately enough that they would recognise the answer as theirs?

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.
