# Product Vision

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 06*

Define what the product should become.

## Product Vision

selfie.Me is a second brain built entirely for the user. **Brain 1** is the user plus their Persona — a living, structured model of them maintained by agents; **Brain 2** is the voice that talks to them through the right persona for the moment. Together they **integrate** the scattered pieces of the user's complete human persona (skill, emotion, relationships, career, learning, habits, and every other part of a life) into a time-aware model of memories, beliefs, goals, decisions, and a versioned Profile per domain. It **enhances** that Profile by comparing it against outside expertise, proposing refinements, and — once accepted — following through with ongoing support so a change actually takes hold. Neither brain has hands: they propose, and only the user's explicit acceptance makes anything durable or acted upon.

## Product Evolution

- **V1: Personal Memory + Evolution Intelligence.** Durable memory, belief evolution, and the first Profile domain refined end to end.
- **V2: Decision and Pattern Intelligence.** Outcome tracking and Profile refinement extended across more domains, each checked against outside knowledge.
- **V3: Personalized Digital Self.** Deeper personalization; the Profile becomes the primary representation of the user Brain 2 reasons from.
- **Long term: a Legacy Experience.** A future generation (e.g. the user's child) asks the Profile a situational question — "how did you get through this?" — and hears an answer in the user's own voice. This is the genuine north star, but it is explicitly not authorized or designed yet: it requires a dedicated consent and legal framework (see `27_Consent and AI Identity Policy`) before any implementation.

## Core Experience

Talk -> Understand -> Reflect -> Compare (against history, and against outside expertise) -> Propose -> Accept -> Remember -> Advise. (Matches the Strategy in `01_Strategic Framework` exactly — nothing is remembered before it is reflected on and accepted.)

## North-Star Test

Can selfie.Me tell the user something accurate and useful about how they have changed — or how they could improve — that a generic AI without their longitudinal history and Profile could not know?
