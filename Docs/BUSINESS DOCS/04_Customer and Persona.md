# Customer and Persona

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 04*

Identify the first user and expansion personas.

## Persona 0 — Founder as User #1

A highly engaged early adopter who records real opinions, decisions, goals and reflections daily, and actively refines at least one Profile domain end to end — a skill, an emotional pattern, a relationship, a learning area, or any other part of their complete human persona. The set of domains is extensible, not fixed; the purpose is to prove that Brain 2 (integrate + enhance) creates value beyond ordinary journaling, for whichever part of the user's life they choose to work on first.

## Primary Early Adopter

- Digitally comfortable adult.
- Already uses notes, journals or AI chat.
- Makes recurring personal/professional decisions.
- Values self-reflection and privacy.
- Willing to correct AI interpretations, and to accept, refine, or reject a proposed Profile refinement.

## Later Personas

- Founder/professional preserving decision history and refining a professional-skill Profile domain.
- Parent preserving family stories and values, and refining how they show up in relationships — an emotion/relationship-domain Profile, not just a skill or career one.
- Knowledge worker refining a learning-domain Profile from accumulated experience and lessons.
- Older adult documenting life history.
- Aspirational only, not designed: a trusted legacy recipient who could one day query a user's Profile — imagined by the founder as a child asking "how did you get through this?" and hearing an answer in the user's own voice. This is a real long-term goal, not a roadmap item: no access model exists for them yet, and none should be assumed (see `27_Consent and AI Identity Policy`).

## Key Validation Question

After 60–90 days: can selfie.Me accurately surface meaningful changes and patterns the user would otherwise struggle to reconstruct; can it propose at least one Profile refinement, across any domain of the user's complete persona, that the user genuinely accepts; and does that acceptance lead to a real, noticed change in the user's life — not just an update to their data — through Brain 2's follow-through (nudges, reminders, check-ins)?

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.
