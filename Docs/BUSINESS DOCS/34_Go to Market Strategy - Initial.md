# Go to Market Strategy — Initial

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 34*

Define how to find the first real users.

## Stage 1

Founder as User #1; document actual usage and failures.

## Stage 2

5–20 trusted design partners who already journal/use AI heavily.

## Stage 3

Narrow early-adopter launch around "understand how your thinking changes over time, and get better at something specific with a second brain that checks the outside world for you," not "AI clone."

## Acquisition Themes

- Personal reflection.
- Decision history.
- Memory continuity.
- Private AI that knows your history.
- Get better at something specific — a second brain that checks the outside world for you and never decides for you.

## Proof Before Scale

Demonstrate retention and meaningful longitudinal insights, including at least one genuinely accepted Profile refinement, before paid acquisition.

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.

## Basis

Grounded in the selfie.Me Strategic Framework and Business Model Canvas, the founder's clarified direction, and the Two Brains / Profile implementation plan formalized in the Architecture, Functional and Technical Design Documents (V02).
