# Pricing Strategy — Initial Hypothesis

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 33*

Define a testable monetization model.

## Free

Limited capture, storage, search and basic memory.

## Premium Individual

Larger memory, advanced evolution/reflection, richer personal AI and higher AI usage, including Profile refinement across multiple domains.

## Family / Trusted

Shared or permissioned family memory capabilities without weakening individual privacy. This does not include Profile access by another person — see Legacy note below.

## Legacy — Future

Optional controlled preservation/access product, blocked on the consent/legal design in `27_Consent and AI Identity Policy`. It must not be priced or marketed before that design exists, even as a waitlist feature.

## Pricing Rule

Do not optimize pricing before proving repeated personal value and retention.

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.

## Basis

Grounded in the selfie.Me Strategic Framework and Business Model Canvas, the founder's clarified direction, and the Two Brains / Profile implementation plan formalized in the Architecture, Functional and Technical Design Documents (V02).
