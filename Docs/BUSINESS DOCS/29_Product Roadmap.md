# Product Roadmap

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 29*

Sequence product capability over time.

## Phase 0 — Founder Pilot

Daily capture, memory, search, evolution timeline.

## Phase 1 — Personal Memory AI

Reliable capture/retrieval and grounded conversation.

## Phase 2 — Evolution Intelligence

Belief/goal/decision change detection and reflections, plus the Profile propose/accept/refine loop and initial follow-through support for its first domain.

## Phase 3 — Decision Intelligence

Outcome tracking, recurring patterns and advisor context, with World Knowledge comparisons extended to additional Profile domains.

## Phase 4 — Digital Self

Deeper personalization; the Profile becomes the primary, user-controlled representation Brain 2 reasons from.

## Phase 5 — Legacy Experience

A future generation asking the Profile a situational question and hearing an answer in the user's own voice. This phase is **gated on a dedicated consent/legal decision** (`27_Consent and AI Identity Policy`) — it is not a natural extension of Phase 4 and must not be scoped as one until that decision is made.

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.

## Basis

Grounded in the selfie.Me Strategic Framework and Business Model Canvas, the founder's clarified direction, and the Two Brains / Profile implementation plan formalized in the Architecture, Functional and Technical Design Documents (V02).
