# Consent and AI Identity Policy — Draft

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 27*

Define permissions around identity, personalization and future legacy.

## Consent Principles

- No identity/voice replication beyond granted scope.
- No trusted-person access by implication.
- Separate permission for sharing source memories vs AI-generated representation.
- Permissions must be revocable where technically/legal possible.

## Identity Transparency

Generated responses must be clearly identified as AI-generated, even when personalized to the user's style.

## Legacy

The product's long-term goal is real: a future generation (e.g. the user's child) asking the user's Profile a situational question — "how did you get through this?" — and hearing an answer in something close to the user's real voice. That goal is genuine, but it is explicitly **unbuilt and unauthorized today**. It conflicts by design with "no trusted-person access by implication" above and must not be implemented as a quiet extension of any existing sharing, export, or family-plan feature. It requires its own dedicated consent primitive and, for posthumous access, an estate/legal framework — reviewed separately, with legal sign-off, before any implementation begins.

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.
