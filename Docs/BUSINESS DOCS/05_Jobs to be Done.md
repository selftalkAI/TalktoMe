# Jobs to be Done

*Version 0.3 | Company Document 05*

Define what users hire selfie.Me to accomplish.

## Functional Jobs

- Capture a thought without organizing it manually.
- Find what I previously thought about a topic.
- Compare my current position with earlier positions.
- Recall why I made a decision.
- Connect decisions to later outcomes.
- Prepare for a new decision using relevant personal history.
- Get better at something specific — anywhere across my complete human persona (a skill, an emotion, a relationship, a habit, a way of thinking) — by having Brain 2 compare my own approach against outside expertise, propose a refinement, and, once I accept it, follow through with support so it actually takes hold.

## Emotional Jobs

- Feel that important parts of my life are not disappearing.
- See the scattered pieces of who I am add up into one coherent picture, not just individually preserved fragments.
- Gain clarity without surrendering personal agency.
- Understand change without being judged by the system.
- Trust that Brain 2 will never act or decide without my explicit say-so.

## Core Job Statement

When I am thinking, deciding or reflecting, help me integrate the scattered pieces of who I am into one coherent picture, and enhance it — comparing my own history and, where useful, the outside world's expertise, proposing what I might consider or change, and following through once I accept it so the change actually takes hold — without ever taking the decision out of my hands.

## Working Status

This is a working document. It should evolve through founder usage, user research, product experiments, technical validation, and legal/security review.

## Basis

Grounded in the selfie.Me Strategic Framework and Business Model Canvas, the founder's clarified direction, and the Two Brains / Profile implementation plan formalized in the Architecture, Functional and Technical Design Documents (V02).
