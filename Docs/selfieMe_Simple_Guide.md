# selfie.Me, Simply

A plain-language guide to the idea behind selfie.Me — no architecture jargon, just what it is and how it works.

## The big idea: two brains

You have one brain already — yours. It decides everything, it acts on everything, and that never changes.

selfie.Me is a second brain, built just for you. It has its own memory and its own way of thinking, but it has no hands. It can never act for you or decide for you — all it can ever do is notice something, suggest something, and wait for you to say yes.

## How a memory actually gets made

Not everything you tell selfie.Me becomes a memory. Reading an article isn't a memory by itself. Struggling through a hard gym session isn't either.

1. You share something — in your own words, a voice note, a video, or a photo.
2. selfie.Me checks whether you've actually reflected on it, or just relayed it. If it's raw, it asks you a question first.
3. Once your own thinking is in there, selfie.Me compares it with what the wider world already knows about that kind of thing.
4. It comes back with a suggestion: here's what I noticed, here's what might help.
5. You decide — accept it, ask it to go deeper, or say no. Nothing changes unless you say yes.

Same five moves whether you're getting better at a skill, working through a feeling, or finishing something you read.

## What "Profile" means

Every accepted memory adds to your Profile — a living account of who you are, organized by the areas of your life you're actually working on: a skill, an emotional pattern, something you're learning, something you read and made your own. More areas can be added any time.

Nothing in your Profile is ever overwritten. When something changes, the old version stays, so the story of how you got here never disappears.

## The two reasons this exists

1. **To help you grow, today.** Your Profile is what selfie.Me uses to notice when you're stuck and offer something useful — never a push, always your call.
2. **To become something you can hand down.** One day, your Profile could let someone you love — imagine your daughter, years from now — ask "how did you get through this?" and hear something close to your real answer, in your own voice.

## What's settled vs. what's still open

**Settled, and already written into the formal specs:**

- The Profile system, how it refines, and the rule that nothing is ever written without your explicit yes.
- The tools selfie.Me needs behind the scenes: somewhere to store your Profile, a way to check outside knowledge, and a way to check back in with you regularly instead of only when you show up.

**Genuinely not decided yet:**

- Letting someone else — even your own daughter — ever actually read or ask about your Profile. Right now, selfie.Me is built so only you can ever see your own data. Letting a second person in, especially after you're gone, needs its own careful decision later — it isn't quietly baked in.
