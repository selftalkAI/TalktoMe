# selfie.Me, Simply

A plain-language guide to the idea behind selfie.Me — no architecture jargon, just what it is and how it works.

## The big idea: two brains

**Brain 1 is you.** It has two halves:

- **You, the person.** You decide everything. Nothing about you is saved, and nothing is done for you, unless you say yes.
- **Your Persona.** A living model of you that selfie.Me builds on your behalf — who you are, the people in your life, your routines, the places you go, what you love, what you're working on, what you've said before, and what has actually helped you. It grows every time you talk, and over the years it becomes something like a second brain: *your* brain, written down.

**Brain 2 is the voice.** It's the only part that talks to you. Before every reply, your Persona tells it who you are and what's going on right now — so it never has to guess.

## Brain 2 isn't just a coach

People need different things at different moments. So Brain 2 can talk to you in different ways:

- **A voice** — like a friend, a big sister or brother, a mother-like or father-like presence, a grandparent, a mentor, a buddy, or a coach.
- **Some know-how** — fitness, food, sleep, money, career, parenting, feelings, cooking, and more.
- **A purpose for this reply** — just listening, encouraging, making a plan, teaching, gently challenging, celebrating, showing you a pattern, or asking a question.

Your Persona picks the right mix for each moment. Tired after a hard week? A gentle, mother-like voice that just listens. Spent too much again? A frank, loving big sister who knows money. Got the promotion? A buddy who celebrates with you.

You can always choose yourself: *"talk to me like my sister"* — and it will, until you change it.

## What a good reply looks like

Because your Persona knows your life, replies fit *you*, today:

> It's a rainy Monday morning, you love rainy mornings and black coffee, and you have half an hour before the kids wake up. Brain 2 says: *"Rain on the window and a black coffee — your kind of morning. Fifteen minutes by the window before the house wakes up?"*

Not a list of tips. Not "I want to acknowledge your incredible accomplishment." Just one short, honest message from something that knows you.

Every reply is checked before you see it. If it's generic, repetitive, preachy, or not about you, it's thrown away and rewritten.

## It starts smart, then gets to know you

On day one, your Persona already knows a lot about people in general — distilled from trusted books on habits, motivation, feelings, communication, meaning and decision-making. It doesn't know *you* yet, so at first it asks more than it tells — one natural question at a time.

Over weeks it learns your routines and what works for you. Over months it notices patterns ("almost every week you missed the gym was a sick-kid week") and shows them back to you — like a mirror.

## You stay in charge

- You can see everything your Persona believes about you, and why.
- You can correct anything — your correction always wins.
- You can ask it to forget something, or to stop learning about an area.
- Health and money details are only remembered if you explicitly allow it.
- If you're ever in real distress, it stops coaching and focuses on getting you real help.
- Brain 2 never pretends to be a real person in your life, and always tells you it's an AI if you ask.

## The two reasons this exists

1. **To help you grow, today** — with replies that actually fit your life.
2. **To become something you can hand down.** One day, your Persona could let someone you love ask "how did you get through this?" and hear something close to your real answer. That's a long-term goal and needs its own careful decision about who may ever see your data — today, only you can.
